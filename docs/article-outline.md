# Article Outline: The Iron Man Suit For AI

## Working Title

The Iron Man Suit For AI Agents

## Thesis

The next step in agentic software is not a brittle scaffold that compensates for today's model weaknesses. It is a model-agnostic suit: a durable exosystem that gives agents tools, senses, memory, verification, safety boundaries, and a way to improve the operating layer over time.

## Outline

1. Models are getting smarter, but intelligence is not the same as agency.
2. The Iron Man suit is a useful metaphor because it converts intention into action through an engineered second body.
3. Most agent scaffolds decay as models improve because they encode temporary workarounds.
4. A suit evolves as agents act because it captures evidence, notices friction, and proposes better capabilities.
5. HyperAgent starts small: Codex, local markdown, mission records, Workshop proposals, human review.
6. The Workshop upgrades the Suit, while the Forge upgrades the Workshop.
7. The durable open source opportunity is agency infrastructure, not another hidden task runner.

## Reader Promise

By the end, a builder should understand why agent reliability needs an operating layer, what a minimal implementation looks like, and why human-reviewed self-improvement is the right first safety boundary.
